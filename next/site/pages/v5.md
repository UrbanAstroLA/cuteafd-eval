# CuteAFD v5.0.0: core precision at long context (study record)

:::answer
**What it asked.** On CuteAFD v5.0.0, how much do the tensors outside the routed experts (the *core*) need their
original BF16 precision? The study ran graded tasks from 4K to 127K tokens on two RTX PRO 6000 GPUs. Two
checkpoints were added during the study, a whole-model EXLR8 K4 quantization and K3.25-Core-FP8, so five
configurations were tested in all.

**What it found.** HQA, the default mode of the HQA checkpoint, did not score differently from the BF16 core at any
length; this is the run's tightest bound. The fully K6 core (compact) and K3.25-Core-FP8 scored lower at 127K and
only there, mostly through replies that ran to the cap without finishing. For compact this rests on four passes
and one interval, so it is indicated rather than established. Every configuration lost accuracy with length, the
BF16 core included. On distance from BF16, which is measured only to 14.4K, the four configurations with 4-bit
experts sit in one band and K3.25-Core-FP8 is farther from BF16 than each of them.
:::

This page is the record of one study. Its results are evidence on questions that other studies also answer:
[what each precision cut costs](precision.html), [what changes at long context](long-tasks.html),
[which checkpoint, at what size](checkpoints.html). The judgements across studies are made on those pages.
Data: [results/2026-11-10-context-probe](https://github.com/UrbanAstroLA/cuteafd-eval/tree/main/results/2026-11-10-context-probe).

## Design {#frozen}

- **Tasks.** 60 synthetic graded items, 20 in each of three families: *join* (match records across the document),
  *ledger* (running totals) and *trace* (follow a chain). Each item was rendered at 4K, 16K, 64K and 127K tokens.
  Scored by [field score](terms.html#t-field-score); a reply that does not finish scores 0.
- **Passes.** The [frozen protocol](terms.html#t-protocol) (2026-11-10 19:55 −08:00) set two single-message passes
  and one eight-turn pass per probe arm, compared by an item-paired difference with a bootstrap interval. Two
  [amendments](#amendment), each written before its own first request, added passes 3-4 and the fifth configuration.
- **Reading rule.** A difference is detected when the 95% interval excludes 0. The protocol named no winner in
  advance and applies no multiplicity correction; each finding below states how many intervals its family holds.
- **Distance from BF16.** A separate stage, planned after the task results were read: [KL from BF16](terms.html#t-kl)
  on the [fidelity set](terms.html#t-fidelity-set) and the [teacher panel](terms.html#t-teacher-panel), both
  stopping at 14.4K.

## Limits {#limits}

- One model family (GLM-5.3-Flash), one engine release, one machine.
- The tasks are synthetic. Each family has 20 items, so a per-family interval at one length rests on 20 items.
- The distance from BF16 reaches 14.4K only. The 127K differences have no BF16 reference.
- k325-fp8's passes ran after the other configurations' and were not interleaved with them, so a session effect is
  not excluded by design.
- The thresholds used to classify how a reply ended were chosen after the replies were read. The per-reply shares
  are published, so other thresholds can be applied.
- The eight-turn pass tests split delivery, not model-written history.
- Not measured: a larger reply cap, other sampling settings, natural documents, fp8-compact and the BF16-core-FP8
  checkpoint on the tasks (their conditional runs were not triggered).
