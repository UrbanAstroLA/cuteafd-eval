CuteAFD v4.0.0: study record

The v4.0.0 rerun (2026-10-30/31) asked whether the release changed anything against v3.0.1, and remeasured both engines on K3.25 and K4, adding K4-Core-K6 on CuteAFD. The release gave the same precision rows as v3.0.1 on the same weights. CuteAFD v4.0.0 was faster than the vLLM fork or level in every speed cell, with larger KV pools, and stayed slightly farther from BF16 on the same weights. The conclusions are on the question pages (precision, speed, running, long tasks, checkpoints); this page holds every finding of the run.

Same-day and earlier data: the top-20 rows of both engines are same-day; the K3.25 full-vocabulary pair takes its v3.0.1 rows from the 2026-10-26 capture, and the long-output text comparison uses replies from the 2026-10-27 K4 run. The vLLM fork's long-output column is from 2026-10-27.
