CuteAFD v4.0.0 against tpurtell's vLLM fork

CuteAFD v4.0.0 gives the same precision as v3.0.1. Against tpurtell's vLLM fork it is faster or level in every speed cell, holds 1.2-1.4x the context, starts in under 100 seconds, and stays slightly further from BF16 on both checkpoints. No reply looped on either engine. Setup: two RTX PRO 6000 only, no DGX Spark expert nodes.

The other changes are a renamed launcher setting and paths for Sparks and other models. No difference is expected for these results; none was measured
