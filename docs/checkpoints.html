Checkpoints: what each one is

The checkpoints differ most in their routed experts, which hold nearly all of the weights, and that is where most of the distance from BF16 comes from (precision, established). Between the two 4-bit checkpoints, neither is closer to BF16 on both reference sets (open). EXLR8 K4 was not detected to differ from the K4-Core-K6 configurations on tasks (supported). The producers calibrated on different text; the teacher panel comes from one producer's evidence set and can favour that checkpoint.

K4 against K4-Core-K6

Between the two 4-bit checkpoints, K4 and K4-Core-K6, is either closer to the BF16 model?

open Neither on both sets. On the teacher panel they are not detected to differ (+0.0016 [−0.0031, +0.0057], full vocabulary), and that panel can favour K4; on the fidelity set K4-Core-K6 is closer (−0.0049 [−0.0076, −0.0026], top-20). By domain, chosen after the pooled result, K4 was closer on legal and reasoning-termination text.

Why this strength: The two reference sets disagree, and one of them can favour one checkpoint. Scope: GLM-5.3-Flash on two RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell Max-Q (300 W); CuteAFD v4.0.0; prefill path to 14.4K.

EXLR8 K4

How does the EXLR8 K4 checkpoint compare with the K4-Core-K6 configurations?

supported On graded tasks over four passes, not detected against anchor (−0.001 [−0.012, +0.009]), hqa (−0.004 [−0.016, +0.007]) or compact (+0.009 [−0.003, +0.021]); an earlier two-pass lead over compact did not hold. In a post hoc look it had fewer replies with every field right than anchor at 64K and 127K (−0.046 [−0.087, −0.004], one of 6 of 102 such intervals). It is farther from BF16 than each probe arm on the fidelity set (+0.0032 to +0.0058) and level with them on the teacher panel.

Why this strength: Added as a reference after the first results were read, then compared under the frozen rule over four passes; the distance comparison was declared before its data. One item set, one engine. Scope: GLM-5.3-Flash on two RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell Max-Q (300 W); CuteAFD v5.0.0; kindlingai EXLR8 K4 against K4-Core-K6-HQA and the BF16-core checkpoint.

  • limits CP-fourpass-exlr8-right — every field right at 64K and 127K (post hoc)
