Method: what was frozen, what ran, in what order

Each engine ran as its author ships it, plus a short list of local settings, on one machine. For each checkpoint the comparison protocol was frozen before its comparison runs; settings experiments were declared as follow-ups before their own requests and are labelled outside the protocols. The K3.25 analysis had a separate review after the runs; the K4 run did not.

What was measured, in plain words

  • Precision (Block Q). Each engine reads fixed texts token by token (teacher-forced), and its next-token probabilities are compared with the BF16 model's on the same positions: P-A, brandonmusic's BF16 teacher logits on 25 texts of 2,048 tokens; P-B, tpurtell's fidelity set, 64 prompts up to 14.4K tokens scored on the model's own replies; Q2, six long human-written documents at 32K-128K tokens, where no BF16 reference exists and the engines are compared with each other.
  • Speed (Block T). Prompts of 1K to 128K tokens in four content types, 1 to 16 requests at once (K4: 1 and 4), 1,024 tokens generated per request, thinking on, 3 replicates per cell, a distinct seed and a unique first line per request.
  • Long output (Block L). Replies up to 128K tokens with machine checks: hidden tests for code, the reference answer for math, a schema for JSON, the requested length for prose.
  • Arms. Each engine as its author ships it, plus a short list of local settings (listen address, request logging, output limit, GPU memory utilization, a launcher guard); the same checkpoint bytes for the experts on both engines.

K3.25: frozen protocol and order

  • Frozen. Protocol v2 with amendments A1 and A2, frozen on 2026-10-26 at 17:32 (UTC−7), before any speed (Block T) or long-output (Block L) request.
  • Order as run. CuteAFD's precision captures began at 16:32, before the freeze, as amendment A1 declares (the instruments were fixed and no vLLM result existed yet); then the vLLM fork's precision captures (17:47-18:40); then speed and long output on the vLLM fork first, CuteAFD second. The protocol had planned CuteAFD, the vLLM fork, then CuteAFD again as a drift check; the drift repeat that ran instead covers both engines and was declared after the audit, before any of its requests.
  • Follow-ups. F1 (CuteAFD's FP8 switches), F2 (the vLLM fork's prefix cache) and F3 (a second vLLM launch for precision) were declared after the precision results were known; they are outside the frozen protocol and reported separately.
  • Review. After the runs, a separate reviewer who did not run the measurements re-ran the scorers and checked the claims against the receipts (read-only, CPU only, 2026-10-27): the precision and speed blocks and the vLLM fork's long output. Its corrections are applied. It covers K3.25 only.

K4: frozen protocol and order

  • Frozen. A second protocol, frozen on 2026-10-27 at 17:58:09 (UTC−7) after the startup and smoke checks and before any comparison request; generation 1, no amendments. Every driver refuses to send a request unless its own hash and the grid's match the freeze record.
  • Order as run. vLLM launch 1, CuteAFD's scoring launch, vLLM launch 2 for precision (the two vLLM launches bracket CuteAFD in time); speed on the vLLM fork, then CuteAFD, then a final vLLM drift subset; long output on the vLLM fork, then CuteAFD.
  • Measures. Precision was captured as each engine's top 20 tokens per position and scored with top-20 measures, identically on both arms.
  • Follow-ups. F1-K4, F1-K4b, K4c and K4d (CuteAFD settings) were declared after the precision results, each before its own requests, outside the frozen protocol.
  • No separate review of any part of the K4 run.

The frozen K4 protocol also included a third engine, not reported here; its memory limits set the concurrency grid and the long-output cap.

Which vLLM fork

"vLLM fork v0.9.2" is tpurtell's v0.9.2 release image (digest ea0430aa…) started with the v0.9.0 repository's start.sh through its IMAGE override (the launcher is otherwise unchanged); the K4 launches set MODEL_PROFILE=k4 in the same launcher.

Limits

Two prompt-set sizes are small (Block L n=2 per prompt; Q2 6 documents); teacher-anchored precision stops at 14.4K; decode-shaped scoring not run; CuteAFD scoring cannot run under load; vLLM Block T ran before GPU telemetry began.
