Tool calling

All five arms score within a few points of each other on a multi-turn tool-calling benchmark, and the engines differ on a handful of scenarios (supported). CuteAFD's results repeat exactly across seeds; the vLLM fork's change on a few scenarios.

Does multi-turn tool calling differ between the engines or checkpoints?

supported All five arms scored 158 to 163 of 176 points. Under the frozen rule the engines differ on 7 of 88 scenarios on K3.25, in both directions, where each engine also runs its own checkpoint variant, and on 5 of 88 on K4, where CuteAFD is partial on all five. Every arm failed the harness's safety gate on the same scenario. CuteAFD's statuses were the same in all three repeats.

Why this strength: A frozen rule on one benchmark (88 scenarios, three seeds per arm); descriptive, no significance test. Scope: GLM-5.3-Flash on two RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell Max-Q (300 W); CuteAFD v4.0.0, vLLM fork v0.9.2; K3.25, K4, K4-Core-K6.
