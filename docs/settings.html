Attention mode (v5.0.0)

What does CuteAFD v5's default attention mode (hqa) trade against a BF16 core?

supported hqa holds 4,266 MiB less in weights outside the experts than the BF16 core over the two GPUs, 756 MiB of it the output head, with no task cost detected (hqa − anchor +0.003 [−0.005, +0.012] over four passes), and is 0.0026 closer to BF16 on the fidelity set. In these launches every configuration was admitted the same KV pool, so the saving stayed unused.

Why this strength: hqa against the BF16 core is the frozen comparison, over four passes, not detected; one item set and one engine. Scope: GLM-5.3-Flash on two RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell Max-Q (300 W); CuteAFD v5.0.0; K4-Core-K6-HQA; graded synthetic tasks at 4K-127K.

What does the compact attention mode trade against hqa?

indicated compact holds a further 2,568 MiB less than hqa in weights outside the experts; it scored below hqa over all lengths (+0.013 [+0.002, +0.024]) and at 127K (+0.037), while against the BF16 core no difference was detected.

Why this strength: As S-long-cost-compact: resolved against hqa only, in passes added after the first results were read. Scope: GLM-5.3-Flash on two RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell Max-Q (300 W); CuteAFD v5.0.0; K4-Core-K6-HQA.

What the FP8 settings cost

What does turning CuteAFD's FP8 settings off cost in speed and memory?

supported W8A16 prefill costs about 4% in time to first token in every cell measured (1.038 to 1.045 on K3.25) with decode within run-to-run noise, and fits every launch measured. BF16 KDA projections and a BF16 head shrink the KV pool: on K4-Core-K6 (v4.0.0) from 1,931,264 tokens to 1,414,656 (KDA) and 1,215,488 (KDA and head). On K4 with v3.0.1, a serving launch with BF16 KDA did not fit; with a BF16 head the planner's dry run fit, and a live launch fell 9,472 tokens short of the context floor. Decode with BF16 KDA was 0.92 of as shipped on K3.25.

Why this strength: Measured in follow-ups on three runs and three checkpoints with consistent direction; speed is descriptive against each run's noise band. Scope: GLM-5.3-Flash on two RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell Max-Q (300 W); CuteAFD v3.0.1 and v4.0.0; K3.25, K4, K4-Core-K6.

Basis (6 findings) and what it does not show
  • supports V3-f1-w8a16-cost — W8A16 first token, K3.25
  • supports K4-f1-speed — W8A16, K4
  • supports K4-c-speed — W8A16 + amax, K4
  • supports FA-kv-pool — KV pool with KDA and head BF16
  • supports K4-b-fit — fit on K4
  • context V3-f1-speed-arms — decode per arm, K3.25

Does not show

  • What these settings buy in accuracy: see S-fp8-settings (precision) and S-think-code-kda (thinking length).

Not measured

  • What these settings do on the decode path's precision: only prefill was scored.
  • Settings on engine versions other than those named in each judgement.
