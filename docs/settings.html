Settings: what each CuteAFD setting buys

CuteAFD has switches that trade a little speed, or GPU memory, for precision. On K3.25, W8A16 prefill closes about 59% of the long-code difference from the vLLM fork for about 4% longer time to first token; with all three switches no difference is resolved, at about 4% slower decode. On K4 on two RTX PRO 6000s, W8A16 prefill with the amax scale rule closes about two thirds for about 4% longer first token, with decode level; the BF16 options do not fit there. Every setting here was measured in a follow-up outside the frozen protocols.
