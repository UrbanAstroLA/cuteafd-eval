# CuteAFD v4.0.0: study record

:::answer
The v4.0.0 rerun (2026-10-30/31) asked whether the release changed anything against v3.0.1, and remeasured both
engines on K3.25 and K4, adding K4-Core-K6 on CuteAFD. **The release gave the same precision rows as v3.0.1** on the
same weights. **CuteAFD v4.0.0 was faster than the vLLM fork or level in every speed cell**, with larger KV pools, and
**stayed slightly farther from BF16** on the same weights. The conclusions are on the question pages
([precision](precision.html), [speed](speed.html), [running](running.html), [long tasks](long-tasks.html),
[checkpoints](checkpoints.html)); this page holds every finding of the run.
:::

v3.0.1 rows from the 2026-10-26 capture, and the long-output text comparison uses replies from the 2026-10-27 K4 run.
